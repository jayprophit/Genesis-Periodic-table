24. Chemical energy / combustion

Boron has been seriously investigated as a high-energy fuel component.

NASA/NACA investigated boron, diborane and other boron-based fuels because of their high heat release and potential aerospace advantages. 

But experiments also found serious engineering difficulties:

* ignition difficulty
* oxide layers
* exhaust deposits
* incomplete combustion
* handling problems for some boranes

NASA combustion experiments on elemental boron show its oxidation proceeds through complex staged behaviour and oxide formation. 

Genesis therefore scores:

chemical energy density: strong potential
combustion kinetics: difficult
clean practical propulsion: condition-dependent

Again:

\boxed{\text{high theoretical energy}\neq\text{easy engineering}}
