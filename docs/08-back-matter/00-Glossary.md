# MAT Glossary

## Purpose

This glossary defines core MAT terms and their intended meanings.

---

## Core Terms

### Material
A substance or entity that is represented in MAT with explicit identity, conditions, and provenance.

### Record
A structured MAT entry containing a material, state, process, measurement, or relationship description.

### State
A material's condition at a given time, environment, scale, and history context.

### Null state
An explicit classification for unknown, missing, inapplicable, or unestablished data.

### Provenance
The record of how a claim, measurement, or value entered the system and from what source.

### Evidence type
The classification of the claim as measured, derived, theoretical, historical, replicated, or other relevant category.

### Confidence
A qualitative estimate of trust in a relevant claim under the stated conditions.

### Uncertainty
The quantified or qualitative range of doubt associated with a value or model.

### Environment
The material's surrounding conditions such as temperature, pressure, atmosphere, field, radiation, or chemistry.

### Scale
The spatial or system scale relevant to the observation or material state.

### Process history
The manufacturing, exposure, transformation, or use history that may alter material behaviour.

### Relationship
A typed connection between records, states, measurements, or sources that captures physical or evidential association.

### Superseded
A record or value replaced by a newer version or stronger evidence, while preserving historical traceability.

---

## Additional Terms

MAT also uses the following operating terms:

- child record;
- parent record;
- derived value;
- model output;
- state signature;
- evidence chain;
- record completeness;
- canonical vocabulary.

These terms are intended to remain precise and consistent across records and documentation.
