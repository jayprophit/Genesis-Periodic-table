# Materials Atlas Table Codex

## MAT
Matter is not defined only by what it contains.

Its behaviour also depends on structure, state, environment, energy, scale, history and time.

The Materials Atlas Table Codex is an attempt to map those relationships systematically.

From atomic identity to engineered material.

From measurement to evidence.

From structure to property.

From process to transformation.

From known materials to possible materials.

MAT is designed as a living scientific atlas: extensible, traceable and open to correction as knowledge advances.
