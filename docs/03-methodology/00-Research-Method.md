# MAT Research Method

## Purpose

MAT is an evidence-first research framework for materials knowledge.

It is designed to separate:

- historical claims;
- interpretive models;
- reproducible measurements;
- derived values;
- theoretical predictions;
- accepted scientific knowledge.

---

## 1. Core Principle

Every claim in MAT must be traceable to a source, a method, and an explicit evidence tier.

A claim with no provenance, no conditions, and no confidence classification is not yet suitable for a final record.

---

## 2. Research Lifecycle

The MAT research method proceeds in stages:

1. identify the material or state;
2. define the relevant conditions and environment;
3. gather sources and record provenance;
4. extract or measure data in structured format;
5. classify evidence and uncertainty;
6. distinguish measured, derived, and theoretical values;
7. validate with replication or cross-checking;
8. record null states and unknowns explicitly;
9. attach relationship graph and process history;
10. review scientific status before promoting to a stronger classification.

---

## 3. Condition-Dependent Science

MAT treats properties as conditional, not universal, unless the relevant condition set is stated.

Therefore research workflows must register:

- temperature;
- pressure;
- environment;
- time;
- geometry;
- scale;
- field conditions;
- sample preparation history;
- measurement method.

---

## 4. Evidence Tiers

Each assertion should be assigned a status such as:

- historical;
- preliminary;
- measured;
- replicated;
- computed;
- theoretical;
- reviewed;
- established.

The classification must be explicit. Historical importance is not the same as scientific validation.

---

## 5. Research Integrity

MAT requires that:

- original source text is preserved when historical material is archived;
- transformed interpretations are labelled as such;
- unsupported claims are not silently promoted to fact;
- conflicting sources are retained with provenance;
- failed or negative experiments remain visible as data.

---

## 6. Record Progression

In practice, a material record may move through stages such as:

- `SKELETON`
- `PARTIAL`
- `RESEARCHED`
- `VALIDATED`
- `REVIEWED`
- `SUPERSEDED`

This progression is operational, not a claim that the material is fully understood.

---

## 7. Scientific Responsibility

MAT does not assume that the absence of a value means zero, non-existence, or a settled conclusion.

The research method integrates this principle by requiring explicit handling of:

- unknowns;
- missing measurements;
- not applicable fields;
- not established states;
- contradictory observations.

---

## 8. Conclusion

The MAT research method is intentionally cautious and structured.

Its object is not to eliminate uncertainty, but to make uncertainty, provenance, conditions, and scientific status visible and machine-readable.
