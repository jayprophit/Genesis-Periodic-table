# MAT Equation and Theory Index

## Purpose

This index tracks equations, models, and conceptual frameworks relevant to MAT interpretation and record construction.

---

## 1. Index Classes

Entries may be grouped into:

- fundamental constants;
- thermodynamic relations;
- phase relationships;
- electromagnetic relations;
- spectroscopic relations;
- mechanical relations;
- kinetic or transport equations;
- models used for simulation or inference;
- historical or provisional theoretical frameworks.

---

## 2. Metadata to Retain

Each equation or model entry should include:

- symbol definitions;
- units;
- assumptions;
- scope of validity;
- evidence status;
- source or derivation;
- record references.

---

## 3. Scientific Separation

This index must distinguish:

- established relations;
- provisional theoretical models;
- historical theories;
- computational approximations;
- empirically fitted equations.

This prevents the same equation or framework from being treated as equally validated in all contexts.
