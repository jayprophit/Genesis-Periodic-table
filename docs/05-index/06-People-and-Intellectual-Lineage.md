# MAT People and Intellectual Lineage

## Purpose

This index records the people, institutions, and scientific lineages associated with MAT records, source material, and methodological development.

---

## 1. Lineage Categories

MAT should distinguish between:

- authors of source material;
- experimenters and data generators;
- theorists and model builders;
- reviewers and curators;
- institutions and laboratories;
- the project contributors responsible for MAT maintenance and migration.

---

## 2. Importance

This information supports:

- attribution;
- provenance tracing;
- historical interpretation;
- review and governance;
- understanding the lineage of concepts and methods.

---

## 3. Record-Level Use

A record may connect to:

- authors;
- institutions;
- associated datasets;
- relevant theoretical lineage;
- earlier corrections or revisions.

This is separate from the scientific validity of the claim itself.
