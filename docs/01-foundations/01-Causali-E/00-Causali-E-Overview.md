# Causali E

## Overview
Causali E is the author's developing conceptual framework concerning causality, constants, differences, potential states and transformations.

The original framework describes three principal conceptual roles:

[
A = \text{difference or causal change}
]

[
B = \text{constant or retained condition}
]

[
C = \text{potential or resulting state}
]

The framework also explores branching possibilities, forward and backward inference, and transformations across scale, time, energy and motion.

## MAT Relationship
Causali E may be used as a conceptual method for asking questions of MAT.

A more general mathematical form is:

[
S_{t+1}=F(S_t,A,B,E,t)
]

This is preferable to assuming that expressions such as A+B=C must always represent ordinary arithmetic addition.

---

## Research Principle
Causali E does not override experimental evidence.

Where the framework makes a prediction inconsistent with validated physical measurement, the prediction must be rejected or the model revised.
