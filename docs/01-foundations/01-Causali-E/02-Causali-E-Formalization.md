# Causali E Formalization

This document is the formal scientific interpretation of the original theory.

The objective is to separate:

- historical ideas;
- symbolic analogies;
- precise mathematical definitions;
- testable predictions;
- hypotheses requiring experiment.

## Conceptual mapping
The original triad may be reformulated as:

- A: intervention or causal change;
- B: constraints or retained conditions;
- C: resulting state or potential outcome.

For a system state S, a general representation is:

[
S_{t+1}=F(S_t,A,B,E,t)
]

where A identifies the transformation, B identifies invariant or retained constraints, and E identifies environmental conditions.

Any equation used in a formal version must define its variables, domains, units and assumptions.

## Scientific use
This formalization does not claim that the original theory is established physical law. It provides a disciplined way to test whether the conceptual model can be expressed in a mathematically valid and empirically useful form.
