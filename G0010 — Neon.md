5. Full electronic shell

The critical electronic structure is:

2s^22p^6

giving:

S=0,\qquad L=0,\qquad J=0

for the ground state.

That means neutral ground-state neon possesses:

* no unpaired electrons,
* no permanent ground-state electronic magnetic moment,
* very high resistance to losing an electron,
* very low tendency to accept another electron into the completed shell.

This is why ordinary neon chemistry is extremely limited.
