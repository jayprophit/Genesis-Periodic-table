$$

\rightarrow
{}^{28}Si+e^-+\bar\nu_e
$$

$$
ME(^{28}Al)=-16850.72\ {\rm keV}
$$

$$
ME(^{28}Si)=-21492.7971\ {\rm keV}
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
Q_{\beta^-}
=
4642.0771\ {\rm keV}
}
$$

Full directed chain:

$$
\boxed{
{}^{28}Mg
\rightarrow
{}^{28}Al
\rightarrow
{}^{28}Si
}
$$

This is stored as two distinct nuclear edges, not one direct decay.

The mechanical layer also needs a strong state/history model. Ambient Mg is hcp with \(c/a\approx1.624\). # Magnesium Causal Material Map

## Electronic

$$
Mg
\xrightarrow{-2e^-}
Mg^{2+}
$$

produces:

```text
closed-shell ion
smaller ionic radius
different coordination
different transport
```

---

## Structural

$$
Mg_{\rm HCP}
\xrightarrow{P}
Mg_{\rm BCC}
$$

Same composition.

Different lattice symmetry.

Different mechanical/electronic state.

---

## Hydrogen

$$
Mg+H_2
\rightleftharpoons
MgH_2
$$

Changes:

```text
composition
phase
volume
electronic state
mechanics
hydrogen chemical potential
```

---

## Superconductivity

$$
Mg+B
\rightarrow
MgB_2
$$

Emergent:

```text
39-K superconductivity
two superconducting gaps
strong B-phonon coupling
measurable isotope effect
```

---

## Battery

$$
Mg
\rightleftharpoons
Mg^{2+}+2e^-
$$

but cell performance additionally requires:

```text
electrolyte
solvation
desolvation
interphase
cathode diffusion
phase stability
```

---

## Biology

$$
Mg^{2+}+ATP
\rightleftharpoons
MgATP
$$

Function emerges from coordinated state, not from isolated constituents alone.
