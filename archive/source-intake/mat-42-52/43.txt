The final Sodium pass closes the remaining atomic, biological and lineage layers. NIST confirms the D₂/D₁ pair at **588.995095 nm** and **589.592424 nm** in standard air, with Einstein \(A\) values near \(6.16\times10^7\) and \(6.14\times10^7\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}\). Na II is a completely different spectrum with hundreds of ionic lines, so MAT keeps neutral Sodium, Na⁺ spectroscopy and ²³Na NMR separate. ([NIST][1])

Sodium also has an unusually strong experimental lineage outside chemistry. NIST's Phillips/Metcalf program used resonant light and Zeeman tuning to slow and trap neutral Sodium atoms, ultimately reaching temperatures below the simple Doppler-limit expectation. ([NIST][2]) In biology, Hodgkin and Huxley established the rapid Na⁺/K⁺ permeability changes underlying nerve impulses, while Skou later identified Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase as the ATP-driven pump maintaining the gradients; the pump should **not** be described as the mechanism directly producing the millisecond repolarization of each action potential. ([Nobel Prize][3])

| ---------------------- | ---------------- | --------------------- |
