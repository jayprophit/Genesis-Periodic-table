12. Why the colours change

When a partially occupied d-shell sits inside a ligand field, the d levels can split.

Visible photons can then excite electrons between available electronic states when the energy difference lies in the visible range.

Therefore:

\boxed{
\Delta_{\rm crystal-field}
\sim h\nu_{\rm visible}
}

can generate colour.

For:

V^{5+}:d^0,

ordinary d-d transitions are absent; its colour arises through different mechanisms such as charge-transfer transitions.

This is why Genesis must distinguish the origin of colour, not merely record the colour itself.

Main G0023 result

Vanadium gives Genesis four particularly important breakthroughs in the dataset.

The first is the oxidation-state ladder:

\boxed{
V^{2+}:d^3
\leftrightarrow
V^{3+}:d^2
\leftrightarrow
V^{4+}:d^1
\leftrightarrow
V^{5+}:d^0
}

where changing electron count produces different visible colours and redox potentials. This is our clearest demonstration yet that the useful electronic identity of a transition metal belongs to its specific state, not just atomic number. 

The second is the all-vanadium flow battery:

\boxed{
V^{2+}/V^{3+}
\quad\|\quad
V^{4+}/V^{5+}
}

where one element supplies both sides of a complete rechargeable energy-storage cycle. 

The third is VO₂:

\boxed{
VO_{2,\rm insulator}
\xrightarrow{\sim68^\circ C}
VO_{2,\rm metal}
}

which couples crystal structure, conductivity and infrared optical behaviour and can be tuned by strain, doping, defects and external stimuli. 

And the fourth is the vanadium-oxide sequence itself:

\boxed{
V_2O_3:d^2
\rightarrow
VO_2:d^1
\rightarrow
V_2O_5:d^0
}

showing that oxygen concentration effectively scans the d-electron population, creating correlated materials, switching materials, catalysts and battery hosts from one metal–oxygen system.

That means G0023 adds a powerful new Genesis design route:

\boxed{
\text{do not only search which elements to combine;
search the oxidation state and stoichiometry continuum between them}
}

because a small movement along:

V_xO_y

can change the material from one electronic regime to another without changing which two elements are present.

The next production node is:

\boxed{\textbf{G0024 — Chromium}}

where the nominal ground configuration introduces one of the first major simple-filling exceptions:

\boxed{Cr:[Ar]3d^54s^1}

rather than the naive 3d^44s^2, opening half-filled d-shell stability, antiferromagnetism, chromium passivation, stainless steel, Cr₂O₃, chromates, hard coatings, ruby colour centres and another major correlated/oxidation-state network.
