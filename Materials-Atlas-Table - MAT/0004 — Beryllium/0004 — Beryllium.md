9. Electronic structure

Beryllium has:

1s^22s^2

At first glance this resembles a filled outer s subshell.

But it is not chemically inert like helium.

Why?

Because:

2s^2

is not a complete n=2 shell.

The 2p states remain accessible.

Therefore:

\boxed{\text{filled subshell}\neq\text{filled shell}}

This is an important Genesis structural distinction.

10. Atomic magnetism

The ground atomic state is:

{}^1S_0

so:

S=0

L=0

J=0

This gives no permanent ground-state electronic magnetic moment.

Bulk beryllium is consequently associated primarily with weak diamagnetic behaviour rather than ferromagnetism.

19. Chemistry

The overwhelmingly important chemical state is:

This distinguishes Be strongly from the heavier alkaline-earth elements.

21. Biological role

There is no established beneficial biological requirement for beryllium.

More importantly:

\boxed{\text{beryllium exposure can be seriously hazardous}}

Inhalation of Be-containing dust or fumes can cause chronic beryllium disease, and beryllium compounds are associated with carcinogenic hazard. 

So the biological vector becomes:

essentiality: none known
toxicity: high concern
biofabrication suitability: poor without isolation
